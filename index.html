About Oanh

I'm a listener
by nature. A healer
by practice.

Hi — I'm Oanh (pronounced "One").
I am a Reiki practitioner and intuitive guide. Being naturally shy has been my greatest teacher — it taught me to pay close attention to what happens beneath the surface: in the silence of a room, the nuances of a conversation, and the subtle language of the body.

Over time, this sensitivity evolved into a dedicated practice. I am a Reiki Master (trained at Om Reiki Centre) and hold Level 3 Pellowah Certification, having trained directly under founder Kachina Maan. My approach is also deeply informed by Authentic Relating and Nonviolent Communication — treating these not just as techniques, but as a way of being truly present with you.

To help you move past the "noise" of overthinking, I also practice Automatic Writing. This certified modality (Level 1, Jamie Natural Health & Healing) allows quiet, grounded insights to emerge, reconnecting you with your own inner knowing.

What I have found, again and again: People don't need to be fixed. They need to be met. When you are truly seen and held in a safe space, the tension releases — and the person you've been searching for is already right there.
